COBALT WORKER - LICENCE, WHAT IT RUNS AND WHAT IT HOLDS

Cobalt Worker lends some of this computer's processor and memory to the training and research jobs
of a Cobalt fleet. Please read this before you go on.

WHAT IT RUNS
- A small Windows app (CobaltWorker.exe) with a tray icon. It starts and watches a private copy of
  Python 3.12 that is installed in its own folder - never on your PATH, never your own Python.
- This installer carries no worker code. Once your partner key is accepted by the Cobalt server, the
  app's bootstrap downloads the worker code the fleet runs, by its version hash, checks that hash, and
  keeps it in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CobaltWorker\build. When the fleet moves to new code it is fetched again
  the same way.
- The worker PULLS its jobs: it asks the Cobalt server for a job, runs it, and sends the result
  back. It opens no port on this computer and nothing is forwarded through your router.
- It works only inside the limits you set on the next pages (cores, memory, hours), at below-normal
  priority, and you can Pause it from the tray at any time.

WHAT IT DOWNLOADS
- The worker code, as above, and only with an accepted partner key.
- Public market data only: price bars and the 15-second tape of futures markets. A data file is
  0.5-1 GB and is downloaded once and kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CobaltWorker\cache.
- Nothing of anyone's accounts, money, trades or keys is ever on the job queue, so none of it can
  reach this computer.

WHAT IT KEEPS ABOUT YOU
- Your partner key (required) and, if you choose to give one, your Anthropic API key. Both are
  stored encrypted for your Windows user only (Windows DPAPI) in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CobaltWorker. The
  partner key is sent only to the Cobalt server; an Anthropic key is sent only to Anthropic.
- The Cobalt server sees this computer's name, its number of cores, its memory and your limits, and
  shows the machine on the fleet page under your first name.

CHECKING
A share of the jobs this computer finishes is replayed on the fleet owner's own machine and the
results compared. An honest machine always matches; one that does not stops being sent work.

STOPPING
Quit it from the tray, or uninstall it from Windows Settings > Apps. Uninstalling removes the app,
its Python, the downloaded worker code, the cached market data, the logs, both keys and the "run at
logon" entry. Your partner key can also be revoked by the fleet's owner at any time, which ends this
computer's access at once.
